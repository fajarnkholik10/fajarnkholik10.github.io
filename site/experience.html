How to read this page

These are descriptions of my professional disciplines, methodologies, and responsibilities—not published client case studies. Client deliverables, proprietary findings, and confidential metrics are intentionally excluded.

01 / Social & audience intelligence

Understanding what matters to consumers.

Looking at public digital conversations to understand audience interests, emerging categories, discussion themes, and consumer questions.

Analytical approach

  • Define a monitoring objective and build keyword/query structures.
  • Organize large volumes of discussion into a consistent taxonomy.
  • Examine quantitative patterns alongside qualitative audience context.

My contribution

  • Collect, clean, validate, and label conversation data.
  • Interpret sentiment and recurring themes without overgeneralizing.
  • Translate findings into concise observations for client reporting.

Typical outputs

  • Conversation landscapes and topic summaries.
  • Audience-interest and sentiment reporting.
  • Research decks with relevant context and implications.
Social listeningTopic & sentiment analysisConsumer insights

02 / Performance & creator analytics

Looking beyond performance metrics.

Evaluating delivery metrics alongside content characteristics, creator contributions, and audience response to understand performance patterns.

Analytical approach

  • Build a consistent comparison across content and channels.
  • Evaluate KPI achievement, engagement, views, reach, and cost efficiency where valid.
  • Interpret performance alongside format, channel, and qualitative reactions.

My contribution

  • Reconcile campaign datasets and verify measurement definitions.
  • Develop performance breakdowns and channel-level comparisons.
  • Prepare reporting narratives and findings for team review.

Typical outputs

  • KPI tracking and performance summaries.
  • Creator/KOL evaluation tables.
  • Campaign-learning and content-analysis reports.
Performance measurementKOL analyticsContent evaluation

03 / Digital monitoring & issue intelligence

Making sense of evolving conversations.

Tracking changes in online narratives and public discussions to help teams understand what is gaining attention and why.

Analytical approach

  • Establish topics and monitoring criteria for relevant conversation.
  • Follow volume, sentiment, discussion drivers, and emerging issues over time.
  • Check notable posts and their context before summarizing.

My contribution

  • Maintain structured monitoring trackers and issue classifications.
  • Identify significant shifts and prioritize relevant conversations.
  • Prepare clear monitoring updates and evidence-based summaries.

Typical outputs

  • Recurring monitoring summaries.
  • Issue trackers and topic-level analysis.
  • Timeline-based observations for stakeholder discussions.
Crisis monitoringIssue trackingNarrative analysis

Understanding how brands show up.

Reviewing channel content, communication patterns, audience response, and use of platform-specific features.

  • Consider audience engagement in relation to content objectives.

  • Compare platform usage and interaction quality.
  • Summarize findings into clear audit observations.

Typical outputs

  • Owned-channel audit reports.
  • Content and channel performance reviews.
  • Prioritized analytical observations and opportunities.

05 / Market & competitor intelligence

Putting brand activity into context.

Comparing competitor visibility, content approaches, audience conversations, and market dynamics without treating volume alone as success.

Analytical approach

  • Define comparable competitor and category benchmarks.
  • Map relevant themes, share of voice, and conversation drivers.
  • Interpret brand differences within the measurement scope.

My contribution

  • Consolidate comparable platform and category data.
  • Identify competitor themes and content approaches.
  • Develop benchmark findings for planning discussions.

Typical outputs

  • Competitive landscape reviews.
  • Brand visibility and theme comparison.
  • Audience and competitor intelligence summaries.
Competitive benchmarkingShare of voiceMarket context

06 / Analytical workflows

Building smarter ways to work with data.

Using practical technical skills to reduce repetitive preparation and improve consistency across collection, classification, and reporting workflows.

Analytical approach

  • Define repeatable structures for incoming analytical data.
  • Apply validation, taxonomy rules, and review checks.
  • Support workflows with appropriate scripts and automation.

My contribution

  • Build and refine data preparation and reporting utilities.
  • Use Python, Google Apps Script, and related tools where appropriate.
  • Review output quality before using it in analysis.

Typical outputs

  • Standardized data tables and reporting-ready datasets.
  • Repeatable cleaning and classification workflows.
  • Process documentation and quality checks.
